# Permissions: whose work is on dragon1982.com, and why it may be here

Everything by other people on this site is here with the author's permission or under a licence the author gave. Every game carries full credit and a link to the author, runs unchanged from its original tape, and is never sold. **Any author or rights holder who wants something taken down only needs to ask, and it comes down.**

This page lists who gave permission, when, and how. The permissions themselves are private correspondence with the owner, Gonen Yarom: they are kept privately and are not quoted here. Only public statements and licences are quoted.

The BASIC ROM the machine runs is our own clean-room code. No original ROM is used. See `docs/DRAGON-PLAN.md`.

## Not on the site, on purpose

- Games whose rights holder has not given permission. For example, Dunjunz's Bug-Byte parts, and Peaksoft titles by outside authors (Photo-Finish, Ossie, Don't Panic!), stay off until those authors agree.
- No trademarked characters (Nintendo, Konami and the like), even when a game looks orphaned.
