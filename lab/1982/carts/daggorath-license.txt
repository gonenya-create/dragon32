Dungeons of Daggorath (1982)
DynaMicro, Inc.: Douglas J. Morgan, Phillip C. Landmeier, Keith Kiyohara; artwork April Landmeier.
Published by Radio Shack (Tandy) for the TRS-80 Color Computer, cartridge 26-3093.

The cartridge on dragon1982.com (daggorath.ccc) is the original 8K cartridge image, unchanged.
It is offered under the licence below, granted publicly by Douglas J. Morgan, president of DynaMicro.
Source of the grant (archived 11 Sep 2010):
