Chapter 15

To the real machine

About 30 minutes · DREAM, tape, Dragon

1Your game, complete

This is the game from the last stage, at “Dragon speed”. Press Run, and move the basket with the arrows (under the screen, or on your own keyboard after clicking on the window). Five apples. How many will you catch?

On the Dragon, a key that you hold down counts once (we saw this in chapter 10). To move the basket three steps, you press three times.

2Typing it into DREAM, on the Dragon machine

  1. Open the Dragon machine: dragon1982.com/lab/1982.
  2. Load DREAM, as in the first DREAM lesson: CLEAR 200,12000, CLOADM, EXEC, and then N (a new program).
  3. Type in the game, line by line, exactly as in the window above. Including the fixed ending, and after it the four houses (SCORE FCB 0 ...).
  4. BREAK and then A: assemble. At the end you should see 0000 errors. Is there an error? DREAM shows the line. Fix it and assemble again.
  5. BREAK and then X: play! Q quits, and one more key takes you back to DREAM.
Want to play again? It is best to assemble again (BREAK, A) before each X. That way the counter TICK starts from 25 every time, like in the window.

3Saving on tape

In the 1980s, programs were saved on a cassette tape. The Dragon machine saves “tapes” too: they appear in its drawer My tapes. To save the game itself, that is, the numbers in memory, you go out to BASIC and use CSAVEM. It needs three numbers: the first house, the last house, and the house it starts running from.

Paste your program here (it is already written), and get the exact line:

  1. After assembling in DREAM: BREAK, then Q and ENTER. BASIC’s OK appears. The game’s numbers stay in memory.
  2. Type the CSAVEM line from above, and ENTER. The tape CATCH appears in “My tapes”.
  3. Check it: type CLS:EXEC &H2EE1 and ENTER (CLS clears the screen, and EXEC runs), and the game runs straight from BASIC. At the end (Q plus a key) it goes back to OK.
  4. To load from the tape: CLOADM "CATCH", and then CLS:EXEC.
  5. To go back to DREAM: EXEC &H6080 (where DREAM starts), and then N.

4On a real Dragon

  1. In “My tapes” there is a 🔊 button next to the tape: it downloads it as sound (a wav file), like a tape from back then.
  2. Connect the Dragon’s cassette cable (the EAR plug) to the headphone socket of the computer or phone.
  3. On the Dragon: CLEAR 200,12000, then CLOADM, and play the sound at about 75% volume. When OK appears: CLS:EXEC.
  4. Does ?IO ERROR appear? Change the volume and try again.

→What now? Ideas to carry on

The game is yours, and you may change anything in it. Some ideas, from easy to hard:

  • More apples, or faster. Change the 5 in LEFT, or the 25 in TICK (in both places). Smaller = faster.
  • Winning. When the score reaches 5, write YOU WIN on the middle row, letter by letter, as in chapter 2.
  • A wide basket. A basket of three squares is easier to catch with. The check LDA ,U will still work with no change: it only asks “is there blue underneath?”.
  • A title screen. The name of the game in the middle of the screen, and “press a key” (the keyboard loop from chapter 10). Only after a key do you start.
  • Subroutines. Put the drawing of the basket and the drawing of the apple into subroutines (chapter 12). The main program will be short and easy to read.
  • Two players. Two baskets: one with the arrows, and one with letters, for example Z and X. Two scores, one in each corner.
  • Sound. A beep when you catch. That is a topic for the next course: the Dragon plays sound when you write numbers to a special house, again and again, very fast.

You did it. You wrote a game in the language the games of 1982 were written in, and it runs on the machine. Every programmer of that time started exactly here: one block on the screen, and then another.

What we learned: assemble in DREAM, go out to BASIC, and save the numbers on tape: CSAVEM "CATCH",&H2EE1,&H…,&H2EE1. Load with CLOADM, and run with EXEC.
From the tape in the browser, through a sound file, to a real Dragon: exactly the same numbers.
The course is over. The rest is up to you.
