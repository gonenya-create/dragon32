Chapter 4

Colour squares: four lamps

About 20 minutes · and at the end: why 255

1A magnifying glass on a colour square

In Chapter 1 you saw that 191 is a red block. But every colour square is really four small blocks, two on top and two below. Each small block is a lamp: on, in colour, or off (black).

Each lamp has a fixed value: top left 8, top right 4, bottom left 2, bottom right 1. Add up the values of the lamps that are on, and you get a number from 0 to 15. That is, one digit of the computer: 0 to F.

2The left digit: the colour

The number of a colour square has two digits. The right one, as we saw, says which lamps are on. The left one says which colour: 8 green, 9 yellow, A blue, B red, C white, D cyan, E magenta, F orange.

And what about 191 from Chapter 1? 191 is $BF: red, with all the lamps on (8+4+2+1 = 15 = F). Now it is also clear why 143 ($8F) can’t be seen: full green on a green background.

3Light them yourself

Press the lamps to turn them on and off, and choose a colour. Next to it you can see how the number is built.

    4The secret of 255

    In Chapter 0 we promised to find out why a house only holds up to 255. Here is the answer: every house is really eight lamps. Four for the left digit and four more for the right one.

    Here too each lamp has a value: from the right 1, 2, 4, 8, and we keep doubling: 16, 32, 64, 128. When all eight are on: 128+64+32+16+8+4+2+1 = 255. There is no ninth lamp, so there is no room for 256.

      Every house in memory is eight lamps like these, even when it isn’t a colour square. The letter A (65) is lamp 64 and lamp 1. The computer doesn’t know it’s a letter; only the screen draws it that way.

      And what happened to 300 in Chapter 0? It needs a ninth lamp (256). DREAM threw it away, and only what was in the eight lamps was left: 300 minus 256 = 44.

      What we learned: colour square = $8? to $F?. The left digit is the colour, the right one is the lamps: 8, 4, 2, 1.
      Every house is eight lamps. All on = 255 = $FF. That’s why there is no more.
