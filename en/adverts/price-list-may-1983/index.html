Price list 23 May 1983

Dragon Data hardware price list, 23 May 1983

The recommended price list Dragon Data sent to dealers: retail prices including VAT, and trade prices excluding VAT by quantity. The Dragon 32 is £175 here; the "Before we made it clever" advert gives £199.50. Page 1 of 4.

  • Dragon 32: £175 retail; trade £121.74 (4–20) or £117.93 (24–100)
  • Joysticks (per pair): £14.95
  • Single disk drive: £275 with controller, £225 without
  • Additional single drive: £200
