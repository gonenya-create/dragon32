Advert Early 1980s

Some new hoops for the Dragon to jump through

Dragon 32 cassettes form a ring around the headline. The advert argues that a home computer’s usefulness depends on its available software, promoting nearly a hundred titles covering games, spelling, mathematics and typing. Cyrus Chess is its main example. The Dragon Data Archive credits the scan to Rob Olive. No date is printed on the image.

  • Computer shown: Dragon 32, on the cassette covers
  • The software claim: Nearly a hundred titles, including more than games
  • Chess example: Cyrus Chess, promoted as Microcomputer Chess Champion
  • The difficulty claim: At level nine, it takes a grand master to win
  • Learning: Spelling, mathematics and typing
  • Seeing the range: An invitation to visit a local Dragon stockist
