Advert June 1983

Our chess program has plenty of mates

A two-page Dragon Data advert from Personal Computer World, June 1983: the Dragon software boxes stand on a giant chessboard, with the Chess box in front. As the archive notes, the middle of the spread was lost in the scan because of the magazine's stiff spine, so a few boxes in the middle are cut off.

  • Chess: "Put together by a genuine Grand Master", 9 levels of difficulty
  • Arcade games: Meteoroids, Astroblast and Cosmic Invaders
  • Adventures: Calixto Island, Madness and the Minotaur, and Quest
  • More software: Personal Finance and Typing Tutor
  • The pitch: "Next time you feel like a game of chess, make friends with a Dragon."
