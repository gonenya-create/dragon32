Leaflet January 1984

Tano software available in January 1984

A single-page Dragon by Tano software sheet dated January 1984. Four games sit alongside VIP business titles and educational software. No prices or storage formats are given.

  • Printed date: January 1984
  • Games: Flipper, Ninja Warrior, Pac Droids, Quizspin
  • Business: VIP Writer, VIP Speller, VIP Calc, VIP Filer, and the words “More Business”
  • Education: Names include Geography Pak, Type Assault, Phonics 1, Phonics 2 and Musica
