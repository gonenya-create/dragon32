Advert September 1983

Dragon! The color computer you've been waiting for

An American advert by TANO, the New Orleans company that made and sold the Dragon 64 in the US, from the magazine Rainbow, September 1983. A drawn dragon breathes fire over the headline, and the copy introduces the Dragon to American buyers: one of Britain's best sellers, with 64K of memory, for under $400. That same month Dragon User reported on TANO's plans.

  • Memory: 64K RAM as standard
  • Price: Below $400
  • Processor: 6809E, "a great advance on the original 6502 still used by our competition"
  • Language: Microsoft Extended Color BASIC, with graphics commands
  • Graphics: Nine colours, five resolutions, up to 256×192
  • Keyboard: Typewriter-style, guaranteed for 20 million key operations
