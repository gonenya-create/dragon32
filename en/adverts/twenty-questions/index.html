Advert October 1983

20 questions before buying a home computer

A Dragon 32 advert from Personal Computer World, October 1983. It invites buyers to tear out the page and ask twenty questions about every computer, from price and memory to keyboards and software. Its answers for the Dragon are yes to the first nineteen, and no to paying for extras to reach 32K of memory.

  • The price claim: Under £180; TV not included in the price
  • The memory claim: At least 32K for the basic price
  • Processor mentioned: 6809E
  • The keyboard claim: Professional quality, guaranteed for twenty million keypresses
  • The idea: Ask the same twenty questions about every computer before buying
