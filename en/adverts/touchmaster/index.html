Leaflet Early 1980s

New! Touchmaster: a touch-sensitive pad

An A5 leaflet for the Touchmaster, "a revolutionary concept in computer peripherals": an A4 touch-sensitive pad that plugs into the computer and bypasses the keyboard. The back page is an order form.

  • Price: £149.95 from Touchmaster Ltd
  • The pad: A4 work surface, 256 × 256 resolution
  • In the box: the pad, transformer, user guides, Multipaint program and overlay, and a stylus
  • Also for: BBC, CBM64, VIC20, Spectrum
