Advert 1984

GEC Dragon at the Earls Court Computer Fair, 1984

A two-page GEC Dragon advert from the brochure of the 1984 Earls Court Computer Fair: a giant "GRANDSTAND", a floor plan, and an invitation to the stand near the West Brompton entrance.

  • Computers: "The ever popular Dragon 32 and the recently announced GEC Dragon 64"
  • Games: "Our vast range of games is here for you to try and buy"
  • Business: the OS9 range of business software
  • Stands: nos. 417, 419, 421, 516, 518 and 520
