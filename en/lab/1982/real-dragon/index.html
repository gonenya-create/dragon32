Loading into a real Dragon

Most tapes in the machine's cabinet can be played from your phone or computer straight into a real Dragon 32 or 64, as if from a cassette recorder. Every game page has a "For a real Dragon" tab.

What you need

  • A Dragon 32 or 64, connected to a TV or monitor.
  • The Dragon's cassette lead: a round five-pin (DIN) plug at one end, and three small plugs at the other. One of them is marked EAR.
  • A phone or computer with a headphone socket. Most phones today don't have one, so you need a small USB-C or Lightning to 3.5 mm headphone adapter.

How to connect

  1. The round plug goes into the CASSETTE socket on the back of the Dragon.
  2. The EAR plug goes into the headphone socket (or the adapter). Leave the other two, MIC and REMOTE, unplugged.
  3. Turn the volume all the way up. On a phone, switch on Do Not Disturb, so a notification sound doesn't land in the middle of the tape.

Loading

  1. Open the game's page on the machine, and pick the "For a real Dragon" tab.
  2. On the Dragon, type what the tab says: CLOADM for machine-code games, CLOAD for BASIC programs. Then press ENTER.
  3. Press "▶ Play". The screen shows S while the Dragon searches, and F with the tape's name when it finds it.
  4. Wait until the end, without touching the phone or closing the page.

It won't load?

  • ?IO ERROR, or the Dragon keeps searching: turn the volume down a little and try again.
  • Check that the plug you connected is EAR, not MIC.
  • On a computer: switch off any sound enhancement or equaliser. Or try a computer instead of a phone, or the other way round.
  • For long games: switch off the phone's auto-lock.

What you can't do

  • Cartridges can't go through the cassette lead.
  • Buzzard Bait needs a hardware key that came in the original box, so it isn't here.
  • A few games can only be played from the site, not downloaded: their authors' permission is for this site.
