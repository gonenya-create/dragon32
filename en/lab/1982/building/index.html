What we are building now

The Dragon machine is a Dragon 32 whose chips are emulated in your browser: the 6809 CPU, memory, video and cassette. The original ROM belongs to its rights holders, so it runs a BASIC we wrote ourselves.

The goal

A machine that behaves like the original Dragon 32, outside and inside: programs are stored in memory as they were in 1982, and original software and tapes run on it.

What there is today

  • The Dragon's chips, through the XRoar emulator.
  • Our BASIC with most of the commands, including graphics and sound.
  • Tapes: loading, saving your own programs, and downloading them as a file.

What is still missing

  • The inside: our BASIC stores programs and lays out memory differently from the original. That is why original 1982 tapes and machine-code software do not run on it yet.
  • Some commands, including the power sign ^, JOYSTK, PCLEAR and CLOADM.

How we are building it

  • The core: a new BASIC core, based on the BASIC that Microsoft published as open source (MIT licence).
  • The Dragon's additions: written from the books published at the time.
  • Testing: everything is checked against what the books describe.

This is a hobby project with no timetable. Meanwhile the current machine keeps working as it is.

Do you have a real Dragon?

If you find a difference from the original, we would love to hear about it. You can write to us through the contact page.
