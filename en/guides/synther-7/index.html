Synther 7: when the Dragon keyboard became a piano

Synther 7 by Daniel M. Stevens came out in 1983. It turned the computer keyboard into the keyboard of an instrument, and you could change the sound while you played. Here is how to play.

Loading

Type CLOADM and ENTER. After about a minute the program starts by itself and shows a drawing of a piano keyboard. Turn the sound up.

The note keys

The white keys are the row ↑ Q W E R T Y U I O P @ ← → CLEAR. The black keys are the row above: 1 2 4 5 6 8 9 : - BREAK. From left to right, ↑ Q W E R T Y are the low C, D, E, F, G, A, B, and U I O P @ ← → are the same notes in the middle octave. U is middle C, and CLEAR is high C. The B just before it is the right arrow, →.

Pick an instrument

GGamba
HHogfife
JNinni
KKrumphorn. It sounds an octave lower
LLute, the standard sound

If the sound goes strange, press / and then L: a full reset, back to the standard lute. L on its own brings back the lute, but after the horn the keyboard stays an octave too high.

Shape the sound

The coloured bars at the bottom left show the settings. Under each bar is a number from 0 to 9, and after 9 it wraps round to 0, so press slowly and watch the number.

A / ZAttack: harder / softer start
S / XSustain: longer / shorter
D / CDecay: slow (at 9 it sounds like an organ) / fast
FFelt damping, 6 steps round
VVibrato depth
B / N"Boing" at the start of the note: more / less (5 = none)
MLouder
ENTER / ↓Whole keyboard higher / lower (the number in the RANGE box)
< / >While a note plays: bend it
SpaceWhile a note plays: the note holds and swells for as long as you hold Space
;Stops a note that keeps sounding
/, LFull reset

First lesson: a tune, a horn and an organ

The tune is the start of "Mary Had a Little Lamb", around middle C: U is C, I is D, O is E, @ is G.

  1. Press L, then press U a few times. That is middle C, on the lute.
  2. Play slowly, one key after another, line by line:
O I U I O O O
I I I
O @ @
O I U I O O O
O I I O I U
  1. Press K and play it again. Now it is a horn, one octave lower.
  2. Press D slowly, a few times, until the number under its bar shows 9. Play again: every note holds like an organ and does not die away. ; stops it.
  3. Play a note and hold >. The sound rises like a space film, and when you let go it glides back.
  4. Finally press / and then L to get back to the standard sound.

You can also play the same tune in the low octave: W is E, Q is D, ↑ is C, R is G. In an emulator on an ordinary computer, CLEAR is usually Home and BREAK is Esc.
