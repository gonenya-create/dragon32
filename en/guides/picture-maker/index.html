Graphics

Picture Maker: the four-colour drawing program

Picture Maker by David Makin came out in December 1987 from John Penn Software, for five pounds. You draw on the graphics screen in four colours, with the arrow keys or a joystick. The full instructions are inside the program itself. Here are the essentials.

Starting

Type CLOADM and ENTER. The load is long, about six and a half minutes. In the first three minutes a picture of a paint pot slowly builds up. After it the tape goes on loading for about three and a half minutes more, with no change at all on the screen. It is not stuck: don't press anything. At the end a text menu appears on a green background:

1Start drawing
2Print the instructions on a printer
3Read the instructions on screen. First a tune plays. Space brings up the first page, and each Space turns to the next

The cursor

Move the cursor with the arrow keys. For a joystick, plug it into the right-hand socket and press J. K turns it off, and the keyboard always works. Holding SHIFT freezes the cursor, which helps when you want to move diagonally without drifting. The cursor moves slowly: crossing the whole screen takes about ten seconds.

At first the cursor is a big cross that spans the whole screen. The - key switches it to a small dot, and back. Switch to the dot before you fill an area.

For the functions marked with a star (*), the current value appears on screen. Change it with the up and down arrows, and confirm with ENTER.

Colours

1–4Drawing colour: 1 green, 2 yellow, 3 blue, 4 red
5–8Background colour, in the same order: 5 green, 6 yellow, 7 blue, 8 red. You only see it when you clear the screen
@Switch between the two colour sets: green, yellow, blue and red, or buff, cyan, magenta and orange

Drawing

The brush works for as long as you hold Space, or the joystick button, and move the cursor.

BBrush type *: pen, rubber, line, box, or a piece of the picture you stored
H / WBrush height / width *
CCircle: the centre is at the cursor. Move right to make it bigger, then ENTER
LLine from the cursor. Move, then ENTER
SHIFT + LBox
FFill an area up to lines in the background colour
SHIFT + FFill an area up to lines in the drawing colour
MMagnify: see the area near the cursor, enlarged

For every shape, ENTER keeps it and BREAK cancels. After ENTER the cursor jumps back to the point where you started the shape, not its end. The author himself advised anyone who isn't a good artist to use the "line" brush: each press of Space draws a line from the last point.

Three screens

Besides the screen you see, there are two stored screens. One is yours (USER), and the program uses the other before some functions (AUTO). That lets you go back to an earlier version.

SHIFT + KStore the picture in the USER screen
CLEARSwap the picture with the USER screen
SSwap the picture with the AUTO screen. In practice it is undo: back to before the last line, box or circle
SHIFT + BREAKClear the whole screen to the background colour. Choose the background (5–8) first
R / SHIFT + RMirror / upside down
, . < >Move the whole picture up, down, left, right

Pieces of the picture

G stores a piece of the picture: move a frame around it and press ENTER. You can store up to 8 pieces. P and the piece number bring it back, and you move it into place. There are more ways to lay a piece down: O, A, N, I, E and T, like the options of PUT in BASIC. V and Z double a piece in height or width. When you start, two pieces are already stored: letters, for writing on the picture.

Saving and leaving

SHIFT + 9Save the picture to tape
SHIFT + 0Load a picture from tape
*Save everything: both screens and the pieces
SHIFT + QBack to BASIC. The picture is lost, so save first

When saving or loading you see FILENAME ?: type a name and ENTER. When loading, ENTER with no name loads the first picture on the tape. Then you see POSITION TAPE AND PRESS A KEY: get the recorder ready, and press a key.

First lesson: a house with a sun

We will draw a house: yellow walls, a filled red roof, a door, a lit window and a sun in a blue sky. Move the cursor by holding the arrow keys, and watch the screen. No need to be exact.

  1. At the menu press 1. The drawing screen appears: the paint pot picture, with the big cross over it.
  2. A blue sky: press 7 (blue background), then SHIFT + BREAK. The whole screen turns blue. Without the 7 first, the screen clears to red.
  3. Press -. The cross becomes a small dot. With the cross, the roof fill escapes over the whole screen. With the dot it works.
  4. The walls: press 2 (yellow). Move the cursor to the bottom left corner of the house, a little left of and below the middle of the screen. Press SHIFT + L and move right and up: a box grows. When it is a good size, ENTER. The box stays, and the cursor goes back to the corner where you started.
  5. The roof, first line: press 4 (red). Move the cursor to the top left corner of the walls. Press L, move right and up to a point above the middle of the house (the top of the roof), and ENTER.
  6. The roof, second line: move the cursor to the top right corner of the walls. L, move left and up to the top of the roof, and ENTER.
  7. Close the roof in red: from the right corner, L, move left to the left corner, and ENTER. The top line of the walls is yellow, and without this red line the fill escapes.
  8. Fill the roof: move the cursor inside the triangle and press SHIFT + F. The roof fills with red, up to the lines.
  9. The door: press 2 (yellow). Move the cursor to the bottom of the wall, a little left of the middle. SHIFT + L, move right and up, and ENTER.
  10. The window: move the cursor to the right side of the wall. SHIFT + L, a small box, and ENTER. Move the cursor inside the window and press SHIFT + F: the window fills with yellow, as if the light is on.
  11. The sun: move the cursor to the top right corner of the sky. Press C, hold the right arrow until the circle is big enough, and ENTER. The cursor goes back to the centre of the circle: press SHIFT + F there, and the sun fills.
  12. Something went wrong? S takes the picture back to before the last line, box or circle, including any fill after it. A shape you have not confirmed yet is cancelled with BREAK.
  13. Saving: put in a blank tape. Press SHIFT + 9. You see FILENAME ?: type HOUSE and ENTER. You see POSITION TAPE AND PRESS A KEY: press RECORD and PLAY on the recorder, then any key. When the save is done, the picture comes back.
  14. Loading it back: rewind the tape to the start of the picture. Clear the screen with SHIFT + BREAK, and press SHIFT + 0. At FILENAME ? press ENTER, press PLAY on the recorder, then a key. In less than a minute the house is back.
  15. Leaving: SHIFT + Q goes back to BASIC. The picture is lost, so save first.

This guide is in our own words, for Dragon owners who have the original program. We do not host the program or its instructions. The keys come from the instructions inside the program, and we tried the main ones on an emulated machine with the same chips.
