Software of the Day

The Dragon was not only for games. People wrote letters on it, played music on it and programmed it in machine code. These are short guides, in our own words, to the main programs of the time: how to start, which keys, and what to try first.

  • TELEWRITERWord processingTelewriterThe word processor with 51 characters per line: the menu, the editing keys and the traps.
  • SYNTHER 7MusicSynther 7The keyboard becomes a piano: the keys, the instruments and shaping the sound.
  • PICTURE MAKERGraphicsPicture MakerDrawing in four colours with the arrows or a joystick: brushes, shapes, magnify and saving.
  • DRAGON LOGOEducationDragon LogoLearning to program with a turtle: the commands, the editor and a first procedure.
  • FAMILY BUDGETHome and officeFamily BudgetA budget, accounts and an address book on one tape, and how not to lose your data.
  • DREAMProgrammingDREAMThe Dragon Data editor assembler, and a first machine code program.

The guides are for Dragon owners who have the original program. We do not host the programs or their original manuals.
